实用技巧Pro Tips

枪法只是基础，信息、道具和经济意识才拉开分段差距。

Aim is the baseline — information, utility, and economy sense separate the ranks.

1急停射击Counter-strafe

移动中开枪毫无准度。松开方向键或轻点反方向键让角色瞬间停稳再开枪，是所有枪法的地基。

Shooting while moving is hopeless. Tap the opposite movement key to stop instantly before firing — the foundation of all aim.

2预瞄头线Crosshair placement

准星始终放在敌人可能出现位置的头部高度，把对枪变成"反应测试"而不是"甩枪测试"。

Keep your crosshair at head height where enemies appear, turning duels into reaction tests instead of flick tests.

3报点与沟通Callouts

记住地图点位名，用简短报点（位置+人数+血量）。信息是团队游戏里最便宜也最强的"道具"。

Learn map callouts and report position + count + HP concisely. Information is the cheapest, strongest utility.

4全队同步经济Buy together

开局按 U 看全队经济，跟着指挥一起起枪或一起环保。三人起两人环保是最差的买法。

Check team money each round and buy or eco together. A 3-buy/2-eco split is the worst of both worlds.

5每图学 3 个道具Three lineups per map

不必背几十个道具：每张图记住 3 个关键烟/闪（如 Mirage 窗口烟），实战价值立竿见影。

No need for dozens of lineups — three key smokes/flashes per map (like Mirage window smoke) pay off immediately.

6听声辨位Sound discipline

戴耳机、按 Shift 静步。脚步声、换弹声、下包声都是免费情报，Nuke 这类垂直图尤其关键。

Use headphones and shift-walk. Footsteps, reloads, and plant sounds are free intel — critical on vertical maps like Nuke.
